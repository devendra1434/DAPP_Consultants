DAPP Consultants is an Australian project management and civil contracting company providing practical engineering, construction management and project delivery services across the civil infrastructure, mining, utilities and land development sectors.

We work with principal contractors, subcontractors, developers, government agencies and private clients to deliver projects safely, efficiently and commercially. Whether acting as a project management consultant or a civil contractor, our focus is on delivering quality outcomes through practical engineering, strong planning and disciplined project controls.
